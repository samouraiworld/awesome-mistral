must be resolved by a maintainer, not guessed around:

- **`mistral.ai`, `docs.mistral.ai`, `huggingface.co`, `arxiv.org`,
  `youtube.com`** (and others) are blocked outright at the egress proxy for
  every tool (`WebFetch` and raw `curl` alike). This is the majority of
  MAINTENANCE.md's own sources of record for news, changelogs, release notes,
